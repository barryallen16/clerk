# Acme Global Employee Handbook & Workplace Policy

Welcome to the Acme Global Employee Handbook.
This document outlines our company policies, benefits, code of conduct, and employment terms.
Every employee is expected to review, understand, and comply with the guidelines set forth below.

---

## 1. Employment Categories & Probation

### 1.1 Employment Classifications
Employees are categorized as full-time regular, part-time regular, or fixed-term contractors.
Full-time regular employees work a standard 40-hour workweek and qualify for all standard health, retirement, and leave benefits.
Fixed-term contractors receive statutory benefits mandated by local labor laws but are excluded from equity grants and company-sponsored sabbaticals.

### 1.2 Probation Period
All new full-time hires undergo a mandatory probation period of ninety (90) days starting from their official joining date.
During probation, performance is reviewed at thirty (30) day intervals by the direct reporting manager.
Either the employee or the company may terminate the employment relationship during probation by providing a written notice of fourteen (14) calendar days.
Upon successful completion of the ninety-day evaluation, HR issues a written confirmation letter transitioning the employee to confirmed status.

---

## 2. Working Hours & Remote Work (WFH)

### 2.1 Standard Office Hours
Acme operates under flexible core hours.
All employees must be available for synchronous communication between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM local time, Monday through Friday.
The standard work week consists of 40 hours.

### 2.2 Work From Home (WFH) Policy
Confirmed full-time employees are eligible to work remotely up to four (4) days per calendar month.
WFH requests must be logged in the HR portal and approved by the reporting manager at least twenty-four (24) hours in advance.
WFH days cannot be taken back-to-back with Casual Leave to artificially extend vacation periods.
Employees during their initial 90-day probation are required to work on-site and are not eligible for routine WFH unless granted written medical exemption.

### 2.3 Home Office Equipment Stipend
Full-time employees receive a one-time remote setup allowance of up to $500 (or equivalent local currency).
This allowance covers ergonomic desk chairs, monitors, external keyboards, and mice.
Reimbursement claims must be submitted with original itemized receipts within forty-five (45) days of joining.
Equipment purchased through this stipend remains company property for the first twelve months of employment.

## 3. Leave & Time-Off Policies

### 3.1 Casual Leave (CL)
Full-time confirmed employees receive twelve (12) days of paid Casual Leave per calendar year.
Casual leave accrues pro-rata at the rate of one (1) day per completed calendar month of service.
Casual leave is intended for personal matters, family events, and short vacations.
Employees must apply for Casual Leave at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance via the HR system.
A maximum of three (3) consecutive Casual Leave days may be taken in a single request.
Unused Casual Leave days do not carry over into the subsequent calendar year.
Up to five (5) days of unused Casual Leave may be encashed at the close of the financial year based on basic salary.

### 3.2 Sick & Medical Leave (SL)
Employees are allotted ten (10) days of paid Medical Leave per calendar year credited on January 1st.
Medical leave cannot be accumulated or carried forward to subsequent years.
If an employee is absent on medical grounds for more than two (2) consecutive working days, a valid certificate signed by a licensed medical practitioner must be uploaded to the HR portal within three (3) working days of returning to duty.
Failure to submit required medical documentation may result in the absence being marked as unauthorized unpaid leave.

### 3.3 Maternity Leave
Female employees who have completed at least eighty (80) days of continuous service prior to the expected delivery date are eligible for twenty-six (26) weeks of paid maternity leave.
Up to eight (8) weeks may be availed prior to the expected delivery date, with the remaining eighteen (18) weeks taken post-delivery.
In cases of unfortunate miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy, six (6) weeks of paid medical leave is granted upon submission of hospital records.
Employees must give formal written notice to HR at least thirty (30) calendar days prior to taking maternity leave.

### 3.4 Paternity Leave
Male employees and non-birthing partners who have completed their probation period are eligible for fifteen (15) consecutive calendar days of paid paternity leave.
Paternity leave must be availed within six (6) months from the birth or legal adoption date of the child.
Paternity leave must be taken in a single continuous block and cannot be split across multiple intervals.

### 3.5 Bereavement Leave
Employees are entitled to five (5) consecutive paid days of bereavement leave in the event of the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, or sibling).
Three (3) consecutive paid days are granted for the death of an extended family member (grandparent, parent-in-law, or grandchild).
Additional unpaid time off may be requested through the People Operations team if travel abroad is required.

### 3.6 Sabbatical Policy
Employees who complete three (3) continuous years of high-performing service at Acme are eligible to apply for an unpaid sabbatical.
The sabbatical duration ranges from one (1) month to three (3) months maximum.
Sabbatical leave is granted for higher education, personal volunteering, or health recuperation.
Written applications must be submitted to the department Vice President and HR at least ninety (90) days prior to the proposed start date.
The company guarantees role reinstatement at equivalent pay grade upon return from an approved sabbatical.

### 3.7 Public Holidays & Compensatory Off
The company observes ten (10) mandatory paid public holidays each calendar year published every December.
Additionally, employees receive two (2) Restricted Holidays (RH) to observe cultural or religious dates of their personal choice.
If a business-critical requirement compels an employee to work on a mandatory public holiday, the employee is entitled to one (1) day of Compensatory Off (comp-off).
Compensatory off days must be formally recorded and utilized within sixty (60) days of accrual, after which they expire without cash payout.

## 4. Expenses & Travel Reimbursements

### 4.1 Business Travel Guidelines
All domestic and international business travel must receive pre-authorization from the Department Head.
Flights under four (4) hours must be booked in economy class.
Flights exceeding eight (8) continuous hours qualify for premium economy or business class booking subject to VP approval.

### 4.2 Daily Meals & Incidentals (Per Diem)
Employees traveling on authorized company assignments are entitled to a daily food per diem.
Domestic travel per diem is capped at $75 per day without alcohol.
International travel per diem is capped at $120 per day.
Detailed receipts must be uploaded to the expense portal within fifteen (15) days of returning from travel.

### 4.3 Internet & Mobile Allowance
Engineering and operations personnel participating in on-call rotations are eligible for a monthly communication allowance.
The allowance reimburses home broadband up to $50 per month.
Claims are submitted monthly through payroll.

## 5. Code of Conduct, Ethics & Harassment

### 5.1 Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)
Acme maintains a strict zero-tolerance stance against sexual harassment and discrimination in the workplace.
Any unwelcomed verbal, physical, or visual conduct of a sexual nature is strictly prohibited.
Complaints may be submitted directly to the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) in complete confidentiality.
The ICC is legally bound to initiate a formal inquiry within seven (7) working days and submit findings within ninety (90) days.
Retaliation against any employee who files a good-faith complaint will result in summary dismissal.

### 5.2 Conflict of Interest & Moonlighting
Employees owe their primary professional dedication to Acme during business hours.
Holding external secondary employment, directing outside commercial entities, or consulting for competing firms (moonlighting) is strictly prohibited without prior written consent from Legal and People Operations.
Open-source software contributions and non-commercial academic lectures outside normal working hours are encouraged provided company proprietary assets are not utilized.

### 5.3 Confidentiality & Intellectual Property
All code, models, documentation, algorithms, designs, and business data created by employees during their employment remain the exclusive intellectual property of Acme.
Employees sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) upon joining that remains in effect during employment and for twenty-four (24) months following departure.

## 6. Separation & Termination

### 6.1 Voluntary Resignation & Notice Period
Confirmed full-time employees who voluntarily resign must serve a standard notice period of sixty (60) calendar days.
During the initial 90-day probation period, the required notice period is fourteen (14) calendar days.
Notice waiver or early release subject to buyout requires mutual written agreement between the employee and the department head.

### 6.2 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
If an employee fails to meet core performance benchmarks, the reporting manager may place the individual on a formal Performance Improvement Plan.
A standard PIP spans thirty (30) calendar days and specifies measurable weekly targets.
If performance does not reach an acceptable standard by the end of thirty days, employment may be terminated with statutory severance.

### 6.3 Final Settlement & Offboarding
Upon completion of the notice period, all company equipment, security access cards, and credentials must be returned to IT Support.
The final settlement (including encashment of eligible casual leave and pending expense claims) will be disbursed within thirty (30) days of the final working day.
